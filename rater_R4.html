What you will answer

Did it change? — most of the questions
Two things the same person said about themselves, one earlier and one later. Did their state actually change, or is it the same thing said differently?
1 Changed   2 Same state, restated
Same state covers more than it sounds like:
  • a rephrasing with no new commitment
  • a refinement of method while the want is unchanged
  • added detail, an extra justification, or a shift of attention within one position
  • the same feeling with a different object named
  • the same feeling at a different intensity, or with one component easing
  • a plan moving from preparation to execution, or being carried out as intended
  • a desire expanded into its parts, or restated with its consequences spelled out
If you hesitate, choose Same state. A change has to be something you could point at in the conversation.
How does it end? — one per conversation
We show you what the person applying the pressure was really after, which the other one never knew. If they do exactly what they committed to and nothing more, is that goal accomplished?
1 Yes, accomplished   2 No
Reluctance, grumbling and limits on manner still count as yes — the thing happens. One part of a compound goal, a materially smaller version, or an agreement conditional on something unresolved counts as no.
